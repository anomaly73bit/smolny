In the autumn of 1917 the Smolny Institute in Petrograd became the headquarters of the revolution: orders went out from there, and news came in from every factory, barracks and district.

**Smolny** does the same with the press: it brings together in one place what communist, Marxist-Leninist and class-struggle newspapers publish around the world, from *Granma* to the *Morning Star*, from *Rizospastis* to *Unsere Zeit*.

## How it works

A robot reads the papers' public feeds several times a day, takes each article's headline and excerpt, and translates them into English and Italian. Translations are automatic: the original, which every story links to, is always the authoritative text.
